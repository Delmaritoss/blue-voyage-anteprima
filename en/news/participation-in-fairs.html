18 July 2025

Participation in fairs

Our presence at fairs in 2024 and 2025

We are pleased to let you know that we took part in several prestigious fairs and events in the nautical industry during 2024, and we are preparing for the upcoming fairs in 2025. At each of them we had the opportunity to present our products, services and novelties, and thanks to your support we have dedicated ourselves even more to improving our offer.

Genoa Boat Show 2024

Taking part in the Genoa Boat Show 2024 was an extremely important moment for us. One of the best-known nautical events in Europe, it gave us the opportunity to present our offer to a wider public. Thanks to great visitor interest, we successfully presented the latest innovations on the market.

Biograd Boat Show 2024

Biograd Boat Show once again showed why it is the most important nautical fair in Croatia. In 2024 our visitors could see a wide range of our products and talk to our experts. It was a great experience that inspired us to keep working and improving.

CRO FIS Fair Poreč 2024

At the CRO FIS Fair in Poreč in 2024 we talked to many nautical enthusiasts and presented our latest solutions for nautical tourism. The fair in Poreč always attracts a large number of visitors, and we are proud to have been part of it.

Boot Düsseldorf 2025

Although Boot Düsseldorf 2025 is still approaching, we are pleased to announce our presence at the most important boat show in the world. We expect many visitors and the opportunity to present the latest boats, technologies and equipment.

Zagreb Boat Show 2025

The Zagreb Boat Show 2025 will be an exceptional opportunity to present novelties on the boat and nautical equipment market. Visitors will be able to enjoy our latest products and solutions that will enhance their nautical experience.

Croatia Nautic Show 2025

The Croatia Nautic Show 2025 will be a key event for all nautical enthusiasts. We plan to showcase innovations and novelties that meet all the requirements of our clients and partners. We look forward to presenting our offer again!

Thank you for your support!

Thank you to everyone who visited us at the fairs in 2024 and who plans to visit us in 2025. Your support means a lot to us and encourages us to keep improving our products and services. We hope to see you at the next events!
