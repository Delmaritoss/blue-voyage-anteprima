The smallest 15 kW diesel generator on the market

The Panda 19i is a new generator in the highly successful series of variable-speed inverter generators. Its rated output is 15 kW (18.7 kVA), with a constant output voltage of 230 V / 50 Hz, 230 V / 60 Hz or 2×120 V – 1×240 V / 60 Hz via a converter.

The diesel engine’s speed adjusts to the load, but thanks to the inverter the output voltage always stays constant. This means emissions and fuel consumption are significantly lower than with a conventional fixed-speed generator of the same power. Further benefits are a pure sine-wave output with exceptional voltage and frequency stability and, of course, quiet operation. The Panda 19i has extremely high output, giving the user maximum quality in minimal space.

  • Extremely small dimensions (650 × 465 × 582 mm)
  • Weight only 160 kg
  • Very little installation space required
  • Extremely low noise level

Comfort on board plays a very important role today and is no longer fully possible without a generator. In the 4–50 kW range, Fischer Panda offers inverter generators optimally adapted to each yacht’s requirements.

The Fischer Panda team counts more than 500 technicians and salespeople worldwide!
