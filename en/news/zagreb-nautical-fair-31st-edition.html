14–18 February: the 31st Zagreb Nautical Fair has been announced

From 14 to 18 February, all the attention of the nautical world in the country and the region will be focused on Croatia’s only continental boat show — the Zagreb Nautical Fair.

In its 31st edition, the most visited and most business-oriented fair in Croatia will present numerous new vessels. A superb offer of yachts, speedboats, boats, kayaks, dinghies, marine and outboard engines, and equipment for diving, sailing and other water sports awaits you, along with the promotion of new boats and nautical equipment on the Croatian market.

More than 300 exhibitors from 30 countries exhibit in seven pavilions of the Zagreb Fair on 15,500 m² of space.

Croatian shipbuilders will present themselves to the demanding nautical market with their strongest line-up and numerous premieres, and alongside the excellent domestic offer the world’s best-known boat builders will also exhibit at Nautika. The fair is known for its diverse offer which, compared with other boat shows in Croatia, makes it the most complete, with indispensable B2B meetings, interesting guests and expert lectures.

Our famous skier Ivica Kostelić, now a passionate sailor, will present his own documentary from the transatlantic regatta “Transat” on 15 February at the fair, then answer questions from the audience.

Interesting conferences

A topical professional programme has also been prepared, including the conference “Skipper education and navigation safety in the Adriatic” and the panel “Women in the nautical industry — She & SEA”. “Opportunities for young people in the nautical industry” is both the topic and the name of networking events that will interest all young people who want to work in boating.

“How to start a successful summer business, how to enter the SUP, kayak or aquapark rental business?” is the name of a workshop for anyone interested, and those after adventure can try surfing on an electric hydrofoil board that has already won fans all over the world.

The marina in the heart of Zagreb awaits you — an event where Croatian business excellence, tradition and experience come together, staged in elegant boats that delight visitors and future sailors. The patrons of the fair are the City of Zagreb and the Ministry of the Sea, Transport and Infrastructure of the Republic of Croatia.

Interest in the fair is also shown by the 25,000 visitors who come every year, and this year too the luckiest visitors can expect valuable prizes.

Hunting and Fishing Days

The Hunting and Fishing Days are held at the same time — an indispensable annual meeting place for hunters, fishermen, manufacturers, traders and associations in this part of Europe. The project partners are the Croatian Hunting Association and the Croatian Sport Fishing Association. Besides the exhibition, the event includes a rich educational and professional programme, with manufacturers and representatives of renowned world brands of hunting weapons, clothing and footwear, optics, fishing equipment and accessories.

As a reminder, readers of the magazine More receive a ticket to the Zagreb boat show as a gift — the coupon is in the new issue.
