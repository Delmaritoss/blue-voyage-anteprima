Your boat in safe hands.

Authorised worldwide service for Honda Marine and Fischer Panda, plus dry dock by the day, month or year.

Honda Marine

Authorised Honda Marine service

BLUE VOYAGE YACHTING d.o.o., headquartered in Rijeka, is an authorised worldwide service partner of the renowned manufacturer HONDA MARINE, one of the leaders in the nautical world.

We operate in ACI Marina Opatija and in ACI Marina Rovinj, one of the busiest marinas on the Adriatic.

Professionalism, responsibility, quality and speed of service have earned us the reputation and trust of regular and new clients in Croatia and abroad.

To stay informed about the latest nautical developments — knowledge we pass on to our clients — we regularly attend service seminars and boat shows in Croatia and abroad.

Our service provides complete technical support with all the necessary accessories, documentation and diagnostic tools, as well as spare parts for all Honda engines.
