Skipper & Yacht Master

CBoat skipper — category C

3 days of lectures in the Blue Voyage Yachting classrooms:

  • Maritime navigation: nautical charts (types, content, catalogues, scales), basics of terrestrial navigation, navigation publications, compass, use of electronic aids on board, voyage planning.
  • Marine engines and protective measures: mechanical propulsion systems — classification, fire-extinguishing equipment, pollution prevention.
  • Seamanship, meteorology, regulations and first aid: national maritime law, means of maritime transport, ropes and knots, first aid, meteorology and oceanography — general.
  • Boat handling and collision avoidance: definition, types of manoeuvre and their significance; rules for preventing collisions at sea; procedures in case of danger.
  • Maritime radiotelephone service, safety at sea: maritime accidents, life-saving appliances, rendering assistance.

Day 4: practical training on the yacht Kigo (Bavaria 49) or, by arrangement, on a motor boat — leaving port, entering port, berthing, manoeuvring, changing course, etc.

The exam consists of a practical part on a yacht or boat and an oral exam at the Harbour Master’s Office, which sets the exam date.

Required documents
  • Photocopy of ID card
  • Photograph 3 × 3.5 cm
  • Medical examination — deck department

AYacht Master — category A (100 GT)

Blue Voyage Yachting classrooms — 5 days of theory and practical lectures:

  • Maritime navigation: nautical charts, navigation, celestial navigation, gyrocompass, radar, hyperbolic navigation, satellite navigation systems, bridge equipment, voyage planning.
  • Ship construction and stability: ship stability, machinery systems and equipment.
  • Ship handling and collision avoidance: definition, types of manoeuvre and their significance; external conditions; handling in various and difficult conditions; International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea.
  • Safety at sea: maritime accidents, life-saving appliances, abandoning ship, survival at sea, rendering assistance, pollution prevention.
  • Meteorology: weather analysis and forecasting, sea movements.
  • Maritime law: UN Convention on the Law of the Sea, 1982; ship’s name and marking, call sign, port of registry; rights in rem on ships; marine insurance.
  • English language: shipboard and general maritime terminology; understanding maritime weather information in English (weather systems, reports and forecasts) and NAVTEX messages; communicating in English.

3 days of practical training on the yacht Kigo: leaving port, entering port, berthing, manoeuvring, changing course, etc.

The exam consists of a practical part on a yacht or boat and an oral and written exam at the Harbour Master’s Office, which sets the exam date.

Duration
8 days (5 + 3)
Required documents
  • Photocopy of ID card
  • Photograph 3 × 3.5 cm
  • Medical examination — deck department

BYacht Master — category B (500 GT)

Blue Voyage Yachting — 18 days of theory and practical lectures:

  • Maritime navigation: nautical charts, navigation, celestial navigation, gyrocompass, radar, hyperbolic navigation, satellite navigation systems, bridge equipment, voyage planning.
  • Ship construction and stability: means of maritime transport, ship stability, machinery systems and equipment.
  • Ship handling and collision avoidance: definition and types of manoeuvre; manoeuvring characteristics of a ship; external conditions; handling in various and difficult conditions; International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea.
  • Safety at sea: maritime accidents, life-saving appliances, abandoning ship, survival at sea, rendering assistance, pollution prevention.
  • Meteorology: meteorological elements, weather analysis and forecasting, sea movements.
  • Maritime law: UN Convention on the Law of the Sea, 1982; ship’s name and marking, call sign, port of registry; rights in rem on ships.
  • English language: shipboard and general maritime terminology; weather information and NAVTEX messages in English; communicating in English; understanding and responding to instructions from pilots and other authorised officials during manoeuvring, anchoring, mooring and unmooring.

4 days of practical training on the yacht Kigo: sailing a boat or yacht — leaving port, entering port, berthing, manoeuvring, changing course, etc.

The exam consists of a practical part on a yacht or boat and an oral and written exam at the Harbour Master’s Office, which sets the exam date.

22 days (18 + 4)

STCW courses

D2Basic Safety TrainingSTCW A-VI/1

The course consists of 4 modules: Personal Survival Techniques, Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting, Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities, and Elementary First Aid.

Days 1–3: theory lectures on Personal Survival Techniques, Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities, and Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting, held in the classrooms of the Mare Maritime Centre, Verdieva 6, fully equipped in accordance with the STCW Convention and the Ordinance on Seafarers’ Titles and Certificates of the Ministry of the Sea, Transport and Infrastructure.

Day 4: Šapjane Firefighting Training Ground — professional firefighters give a short theory session, and the rest is practical training in which all participants go through a unit simulating burning ship compartments.

Day 5: Bakar Maritime School training ground — lifeboat launching exercises (SOHE). Afterwards, participants visit a Jadrolinija catamaran in Rijeka to get to know the emergency exits, life-saving equipment, etc.

Day 6: Mare Maritime Centre — theory and practice of Elementary First Aid.

Duration
50 hours (6 days)
Required documents
  • Photocopy of ID card
  • Photograph 3 × 3.5 cm

Before you enrol

  • Attendance at lectures is mandatory. If you do not attend classes, Blue Voyage Yachting will not issue a course-completion certificate, in accordance with the MMPI ordinance.
  • Information on the documents needed to register for the exam and on payment for courses and exams is given on the first day of the course. Once all documents are collected, we register you for the exams at the Rijeka Harbour Master’s Office.
  • The course-completion certificate is issued upon payment.
  • If the certificate is lost, PCM j.d.o.o. will issue a new one upon proof of payment of EUR 2.65.
  • We cannot register you for the exam with incomplete documentation.
