Description

If you are looking to experience the beauty of the Adriatic sea on a modern, traditional gulet, then gulet Andjeo is the right gulet for you. It is a boat full of surprises. On the outside, it seems like a beautiful, well maintained gulet. However, once you see the interior, you will be convinced that you are standing in a top class modern yacht. One of the best maintained gulet on the Adriatic is newly refitted in 2009 and offers crew flexibility and will meet anyones standards and way of travel.

Guest cabin configuration: 1 master cabin, 4 double cabins

Each of the cabins has wardrobe, 24 V/220 electricity, hair dryers, its own en-suite bathroom, home like, with shampoo, toilet paper, soap, shower: out of wall.

Beach towels: 1 per person per week

Bathroom towels: 2 towels per person per week.

  • Additional beach or bathroom towels: on request
  • Bedding info: bedding sheets are included in price and can be changed 1 per week

Air-condition: 24 hours/ day is included in the price.

Deck

The back of the ship has a hard top covered dining area with dining table and chairs, combined with a big cushioned sofa. The front of the boat features a large area with sun mattresses and comfortable sofa.

Salon areas

The salon is an exquisite combination of modern and traditional, with highly polished dark and light wood. The salon has a dining table and sofa, as well as the boat bar and the galley. The corridors lead to 4 cabins in the front of the ship and 1 cabin in the back. The cabins are unique, different from any other gulet in the Adriatic, with a very contemporary look and lovely decorated bathrooms

Captain Dragan, a well known and respected captain, famous for his love for the boat he owns. He takes pride in maintaining the boat to perfection, and he regularly invests into improving his gulet and his services every year. Two crew members are a professional chef and a very experienced, friendly sailor/first mate. They will do their best to give you the experience of a life time, with incredible food, and beautiful sights.

Accommodation on the yacht, 3 crew members, wages and food for the crew, Croatian VAT, unlimited cruising hours per day, fuel for generator use, 5 m tender 100 HP, complete linen and towels, yacht insurance, service on board, sojourn taxes, yacht cleaning, use of leisure equipment on board, WiFi.
