Welcome to the Lagoon 52 ft “AKANA”! This luxurious yacht is available for charter in Croatia and offers all the amenities you could need for a relaxing and enjoyable vacation on the water.

The “AKANA” was built in 2019 and is equipped with a generator and air conditioning to keep you comfortable in any weather. It also features a water maker, ice maker, and coffee machine to help you stay refreshed and energized throughout your trip.

– USD 4.500 paid in advance for covering running costs during the charter: port and marina fee, water toys, national park entrances, custom fees and charter licenses if chartered outside of Croatia, drinks which are not included in beverage packages, and anything else that guests might need while on charter. A strict set of accounts is held by the captain throughout the charter and any funds not spent are refunded to the client in full.

Price includes

Captain & Stewardess / preparation of breakfast and one additional simple meal per day (lunch or dinner)
