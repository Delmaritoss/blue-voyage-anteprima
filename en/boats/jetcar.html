Features

Car-Like Design
Shaped to resemble a sports car or vehicle, offering a sleek and eye-catching appearance. Detailed interiors with comfortable seating and a dashboard-like console for controls.
Jet Propulsion
Powered by a water-jet propulsion system for smooth and speedy performance on the water. Efficient and maneuverable, suitable for lakes, rivers, and calm coastal waters.
Custom Build Options
High-end materials and finishes to match luxury aesthetics. Custom paint, decals, and interior configurations for personalization.
Safety Features
Stable hull design to prevent capsizing and ensure smooth rides. Equipped with life-saving tools like navigation lights, life jackets, and emergency signaling devices.
Use Cases
Perfect for recreational fun and attracting attention at marinas. Can be part of a yacht’s water toy collection for added entertainment.
