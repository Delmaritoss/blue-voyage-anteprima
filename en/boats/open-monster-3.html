Description

Monster 3 stands out for its elegant lines and solutions that, according to the shipyard, will offer an unparalleled sailing experience. The exterior, waterlines, and interior design of Monster 3 are by the Nautech Design Studio in collaboration with the technical department of Munstago Custom Yacht.

The aft cockpit, equipped with folding bulwarks, offers 360-degree views of the stern. The large sundeck and outdoor sofa ensure excellent comfort, allowing you to relax and admire the seascape in complete serenity.

The bow of the vessel features a comfortable sundeck, perfect for those who want to tan and relax in the sun. The interior of Monster 3 includes two cabins that can accommodate up to four people and a comfortable bathroom. The classic finishes with a modern twist, combined with the use of high-tech materials, give Monster 3 a sophisticated and innovative atmosphere.
