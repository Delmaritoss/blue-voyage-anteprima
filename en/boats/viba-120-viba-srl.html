Description

  • The multifunctional heart of the fleet
  • Operational, modular, ready for anything. The

Viba 120 – Viba Srl is one of the most flexible and sought-after vessels. Its 12-meter length offers ample maneuvering space for both crew and guests. The vessel can be configured for professional activities such as passenger transport, diving, fishing, or floating catering, without compromising comfort and safety. Ideal for those seeking a work vessel that can adapt to multiple operational needs.

This type of “ESSENTIAL” set-up is intended for those owners who want to experience the sea with a “HIT AND RUN” philosophy, essential, minimal but functional, with no shortage of services and spaces on board available for fishing or recreational activities.

Luxury

This type of vessel is intended for owners who, in addition to needing space, comfort, and elegance, also aim to have greater onboard comfort and more welcoming furnishings to share with their guests or crew.

Deluxe

This type of vessel represents the top of the range. In addition to all the requirements present in the LUXURY trim levels, they are differentiated by a more elegant and refined layout, more modern and technologically advanced on-board systems, ease of management of the vessel, but above all they offer the possibility of high-level customizations according to the customer’s requests and needs.
