Features

Custom-Built for Group Activities
Designed for day trips or longer cruises, often accommodating larger groups. Tailored layouts with spacious decks for sightseeing, lounging, or dining.
Purpose-Built for Excursions
Ideal for private tours, group charters, or commercial excursion operators in coastal regions like the Aegean and Mediterranean. Can be configured for water sports, snorkeling, or diving activities.
Sustainability and Quality Materials
Use of premium materials and eco-friendly design principles to ensure longevity and environmental compliance.

Description

Traditional and Modern Design

Many excursion boats reflect the traditional Turkish gulet style, characterized by wooden finishes and elegant craftsmanship. Modern amenities like air conditioning, entertainment systems, and luxury furnishings are often included.
