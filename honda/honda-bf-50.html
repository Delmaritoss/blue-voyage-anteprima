Opis i tehnički podaci

Models
BF 50 SRT, BF 50 LRT, BF 50 SH, BF 50 LH
  • Explanation
  • S: Short shaft, L: Long shaft

RT: Trim hydraulics, side remote control, tachometer, trim indicator, ALU prop and 25l fuel tank.

Technical data:

Engine
4-stroke OHC, 808 cm3, 3 cylinders, 40HP/29.8kW at 6000 U/min.,

Water cooling, PGM-FI programmed fuel injection, electronic ignition, exhaust through the propeller,

Spark plug: Hoinda 98069-577P (NGK DR7EB), engine oil: 2.4 L, foot oil: 0.52 L

Built-in BLAST system. Adapted to the possibility of using the NMEA 2000 system.

Gearbox
Gear ratio 2.09:1, forward – neutral – reverse
Equipment
LRTD-Propeller 11 1/4 × 13, oil warning light, switch

For extinguishing (Quickstop), electro stert, electro trim or manual with the help of a gas shock absorber,

Rev limiter, 12V/17A Charging coil.

Dimensions
Length/Width/Height: 685mm/370mm/1255mm(S), 1360(L) mm
  • Mirror height: 416mm (S), 521mm (L)
  • Weight: 88kg (S), 90kg (L)
  • Arguments when selling:
Performance
3 cylinders – 888 cm3: the best power-to-weight ratio, the lightest engine in its class, lighter than

Of a comparable 2-stroke engine.

Reliability
High-quality and robust construction enables the longevity of the engine, alarm in case

Overheating, oil pressure indicator, rev limiter.

Frugality
50% lower consumption than in a 2-stroke engine, average consumption: 5.8 l/h
Practicality
Vibrating arm over perfect motor mount, reduced noise over large

Intake silencer, electric starter, electric trim & tilt (LRTG).

Environmental Protection
Meets EPA 2006 and CARB 2008 standards
