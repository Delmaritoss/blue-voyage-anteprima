Opis i tehnički podaci

HONDA’S FIRST V8

Honda’s first production V8 engine is designed to deliver maximum power with impressive fuel efficiency.

Models
BF 350XD/UD

Engine

V8 outboard engine, 350 horse power (261 kW) from 5 liters (capacity 4952 cc), 32 valves, 60 degrees with VTEC technology that is characteristic of all top Honda engines, i.e. Variable lift and timing control system.

A well-established feature of Honda outboards, ECOmo mode is a fuel management system that optimizes fuel consumption. The electronic control unit, when cruising at a constant speed, automatically adjusts the air-fuel mixture according to speed and load, allowing gasoline use to be reduced when possible. An important role is also played by the O2 sensor, which optimizes gasification according to the amount of residual oxygen detected in the exhaust gases.

Auto-trim function: just press twice to automatically tilt the entire engine without the need for a long press. In combination, Tilt Limit comes into play with which the owner can select the maximum tilt angle and thus avoid engine damage during tie-down operations. Simply push the button and the motor will stop at the preset position.

Honda’s exclusive VTEC technology offers the ultimate combination of power, torque and fuel economy.

Honda’s exclusive BLAST system improves acceleration at low speeds.

Optimized fuel efficiency extends range and lowers operating costs, outperforming competitive cruise fuel efficiency by 17% and cost by 20%.

IST: EXCELLENT CONTROL

Honda’s exclusive iST® (Intelligent Shift and Throttle®) drive-by-wire system provides superior, effortless throttle and shift control, plus plug-and-play installation of the Optimus 360 by Dometic® joystick system.

Design – available in two colors

This engine has very modern and sporty lines, with an interplay of curves that give dynamics and penetration. Honda’s new 350-horsepower outboard has tapered lines and is available in Silver Aquamarine or Grand Prix White with chrome and three-dimensional logos.

Equipment
Propeller (option), oil control light with warning, shut-off switch (Quickstop),
  • Electric start, electric trim, integrated fuel separator, rev limiter,
  • 12V-70A/93A Charging coil, remote controls and instrument set
