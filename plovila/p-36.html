P 36 je namjenski brod za parasailing: hidraulično vitlo, stup za vuču, prostrana paluba i stabilan trup za do 16 osoba i 43 čvora.

Značajke

Specialized Towing Mechanism
Equipped with a robust hydraulic winch system to deploy and retrieve the parasail safely. Towing pylon designed for optimal lift and stability during parasailing.
Spacious and Functional Deck Design
Ample space for passengers, crew, and parasailing equipment. Storage compartments for harnesses, life vests, and other gear. Non-slip decking for safety during operations.
High-Performance Hull
Designed for stability and smooth operation in various sea conditions. Hulls are typically made of fiberglass or aluminum for durability and reduced weight.
Powerful Engine System
Equipped with outboard or inboard engines providing the necessary power to tow parasailers efficiently. Reliable performance ensures consistent and safe operations.
Safety Features
Passenger safety rails and padded seating areas. Redundant systems for winch operation and emergency scenarios.
Customization
Options for branding, seating arrangements, and additional water sports equipment. Some models include luxury features for private use.
