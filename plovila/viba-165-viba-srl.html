The flagship of the VIBA fleet

The flagship of the VIBA fleet can be configured for clam fishing. This model was born from our experience in the sector and the desire to offer a highly specialized solution for marine professionals. The vessel is equipped with advanced technologies and an optimized design to ensure efficiency, safety, and productivity during fishing operations. This particular version of the

Achieves the perfect balance between innovation and maritime tradition, allowing fishermen to operate effectively and sustainably.
