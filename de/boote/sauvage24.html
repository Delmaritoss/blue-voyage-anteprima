Herstellerbeschreibung (auf Englisch)

The vessel RANIERI SAUVAGE 24 Sundeck is a boat of CONTEMPORARY DESIGN that combines three types of vessels:

– First of all, it is an intelligently designed compact boat with a cabin – a sufficiently large Sundeck boat with a comfortable sunbathing area on the bow

Modern fisherman

The deck is spacious and provides comfortable movement and space for lounging in the sun. Under the deck there is a cabin with beds for at least 2 people. The cockpit offers a wide bench at the stern, under which there is a large storage area. Side aisles are wide and safe. Bench covers and sun cushions are standard equipment on the vessel. Ranieri SAUVAGE 24 is a perfect family boat with which you can enjoy various activities such as water sports and cruising along the Adriatic coast and get to know our islands and beautiful bays.
