Herstellerbeschreibung (auf Englisch)

Euphoria II offers guest accommodation for up to 12 guests in 5 suites comprising a master suite, two double cabins and two twin cabins. She is also capable of carrying up to 9 crew onboard to ensure a relaxed luxury yacht charter experience.

On your charter, you’ll find plenty to keep you busy and entertained including a deck jacuzzi, perfect to enjoy the scenery with your favourite drink in hand.

Whatever your activities on your charter, you’ll find some impressive features are seamlessly integrated to help you including the underwater lights, adding spectacle and style after dark. Guests will experience complete comfort while chartering thanks to air conditioning.

Euphoria II is built with a steel hull and aluminium superstructure. Powered by twin Caterpillar engines, she comfortably cruises at 12 knots, reaches a maximum speed of 16 knots with a range of up to 4,500 nautical miles from her 85,000 litre fuel tanks at cruising speed. An advanced stabilisation system on board reduces the side-to-side roll of the yacht and promises guests exceptional comfort levels at anchor or when underway.

Book your next the Mediterranean luxury yacht charter aboard Euphoria II this summer. She is also accepting bookings this winter on request.
