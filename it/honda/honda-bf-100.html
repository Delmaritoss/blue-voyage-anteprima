Explanation
L: Long axle, X: Extra long axle, RT: Remote control and hydraulic trim.

Technical data:

Engine
4-stroke OHC, 1496 cm3, 4 cylinders, 100HP/73.6kW at 5500 – 6300 U/min.,

Water cooling, PGM-FI programmed injection, electronic ignition, exhaust through a propeller,

Engine oil: 4 l, foot oil: 0.95 L

VTEC – variable valve timing and electronic lift control.

Built-in BLAST system – system of additional power at low engine speeds.

Engine adapted to the NMEA 2000 standard.

Gearbox
Gear ratio 2.33:1, forward – neutral – reverse
Equipment
Propeller (option), oil control light with warning, switch

For extinguishing (Quickstop), electro stert, electro trim,

Rev limiter, 12V-44A Charging coil.
