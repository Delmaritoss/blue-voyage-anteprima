Models

  • L: Long shaft, X: Extra long shaft, XX: Ultra long shaft, D: European type,

C: Counterrotation (propeller turning to the left) in twin engines.

Technical data:

Engine
4-stroke DOHC, 2354 cm3, 4 cyl., 150 HP/110.3 kW at 6000 U/min.,
  • Water cooling, sequential injection electronic direct ignition, variable
  • Air intake, propeller through exhaust, spark plug: NGK IZFR 6 K11,
  • VTEC: Variable valve Timing and lift Electronically Controlled / 16 valves
  • Engine oil: 7.5 L, foot oil: 0.98 l/h

Built-in BLAST system, possibility of electronic control.

Engine adapted to the NMEA 2000 standard

Gearbox
Gear ratio 1.87:1, forward – neutral – reverse
Equipment
Propeller (option), oil control light with warning, switch
  • For extinguishing (Quickstop), electro stert, electro trim, integrated fuel separator,
  • Rev limiter, 12V-40A charging coil, remote controls and instrument set
Dimensions
Length/Width/Height: 845mm/580mm/1665mm (L), 1795mm (X)
  • Mirror height: 508mm (L), 635mm (X)
  • Weight: 214kg (L), 217Kg (X)

Performance
Top speed and acceleration like a 2-stroke engine, better power output over a wider range

Of revolutions, large volume and variable air intake: the highest torque in its class at

Full throttle (maximum torque of 3000 U/Min.).

  • VTEC and DOHC VETC in combination: Double Over Head Camshaft (two camshafts)
  • 3 special cooling circuits: Head, block and foot
  • The engine is the same as the new Accord
Practicality
