Descrizione del costruttore (in inglese)

One Blue is a custom made motor yacht ready for charter in Croatia. The yacht was built by Oceanline Shipyard in 2009 but with its amazing timeless design the yacht remains one of the top charters in Croatia. This luxury vessel’s sophisticated exterior design and engineering are the work of Mulder Design.

One Blue yacht charter holds up to 12 guests in 1 master suite, 1 VIP stateroom, 2 double, and 2 twin cabins.

The yacht charter comes with 6 yacht crew onboard to ensure that guest’s stay is stress free and just how the guests envisioned their Croatian vacation. Interior design is truly stylish making every stay comfortable. One Blue is truly luxury charter option made for those not accepting any compromises and the ones looking for true designer looking yacht.

Of charter price paid with the balance for covering running costs during the charter: port and marina fee, water toys, national park entrances, custom fees and charter licenses if chartered outside of Croatia, drinks which are not included in beverage packages, and anything else that guests might need while on charter. A strict set of accounts is held by the captain throughout the charter and any funds not spent are refunded to the client in full.

Charter is only available for weekly charter. For shorter stay the owner might allow exceptions, please contact us for special requests. 50% deposit needed to secure the trip and other 50% is due 60 days prior to the arrival.
