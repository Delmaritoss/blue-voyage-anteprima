Welcome to the Bali 5.4 “Namaste of Bali” crewed catamaran, a luxurious and well-equipped vessel that is part of the fleet of Adriatic Sailing yacht charter company in Croatia.

The Bali 5.4 is a spacious and modern catamaran, with plenty of room for you and your guests to relax and unwind as you explore the stunning Adriatic Sea. The boat features 6 comfortable cabins, each with its own private bathroom, and a generous main salon and cockpit area where you can sit back and take in the breathtaking views

On board the Bali 5.4, you will be taken care of by an experienced and friendly crew of 2, including the Captain and a Stewardess/ Cook/Deck-Hand.

– USD 9.000 paid in advance for covering running costs during the charter: port and marina fee, water toys, national park entrances, custom fees and charter licenses if chartered outside of Croatia, drinks which are not included in beverage packages, and anything else that guests might need while on charter. A strict set of accounts is held by the captain throughout the charter and any funds not spent are refunded to the client in full.

Price includes

Captain & Stewardess / preparation of breakfast and one additional simple meal per day (lunch or dinner)
