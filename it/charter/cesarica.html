Descrizione del costruttore (in inglese)
  • Fly bridge
  • : Yes

Motor Yacht Cesarica is a new all-wooden luxury motor yacht, built in 2013. The gulet is 41 meters long overall, 9 meters wide, with two masts and three decks, can accommodate up to 24 persons. All cabins are spacious and constructed of mahogany walls and floors with private bathroom. With numerous additional boat features on different decks (outdoor bar, jacuzzi, huge sundeck for sunbathing) and with lots of additional waters sports, guests will definitely find something to enjoy.

When Cesarica was just a dream, she was envisioned as a luxury yacht not yet seen in Croatia at the time, a completely wooden luxury motor sailing yacht with comfort of the passenger in mind. The vision was to build a large boat with a small number of cabins for its size to accommodate just the right number of guests where each guest can feel appreciated and attended to. Cesarica has a small boat feel with the luxury of a yacht. It was constructed with the highest quality materials such as teak, mahogany, and oak, which give it an old-world feel yet the granite, marble and stainless steel give it a modern elegant look. The cabins are spacious but charming and tastefully decorated. The kitchen is large and of restaurant quality where the chef will be able to create fresh Croatian specialties. Great food, comfort, and uncompromising customer service is something to expect when chartering this vessel.

A weeks accommodation on the yacht, full crew, basic room cleaning service, linens and towels, one beach towel per guest/week, welcome drink, Jacuzzi, use of tender for transport to/from port, 4 hours of navigation per day and use of basic water sports equipment (that don’t require use of tender). Air conditioning use as needed during the day (use restricted after midnight). WiFi Internet service in salon.
