Descrizione del costruttore (in inglese)

As with the Navetta Voyager 46, the new Navetta Voyager 54 Dual Wave is based on a hull originally conceived for professional boats, suitable for those who spend much of their lives at sea, and redefines the limits of design.

A Transformer can be installed in the stern upon request, and the swim platform can accommodate a tender up to 3 meters in length. Unique in its range, the vessel features a pool with hydromassage in the stern.

The vessel features a flybridge of approximately 25 square meters with a second helm station.

A new interior design

The boat’s layout features a galley, dining area, lounge, and helm station on the main deck, and three cabins and two heads on the lower deck.

The generous hull volume and headroom make the lower deck welcoming, spacious, and comfortable.
