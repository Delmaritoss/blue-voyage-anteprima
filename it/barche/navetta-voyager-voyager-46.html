Descrizione del costruttore (in inglese)

The new Navetta Voyager 46 is based on a hull originally conceived for professional boats, suitable for those who spend much of their lives at sea, and redefines the limits of design.

Thanks to the large aft cockpit, connected to the swim platform by a seating system, and the bow sundeck, the vessel offers over 40 square meters of outdoor space with direct access to the sea.

Furthermore, upon request, a Transformer can be installed in the aft deck, and the swim platform can accommodate a tender up to 3 meters in length.

The vessel also features a flybridge of approximately 10 square meters with an integrated sundeck and the option of adding a second helm station.

A new interior design

The boat’s layout features a lounge area and helm station on the main deck and two cabins and a bathroom on the lower deck.

The generous hull volume and headroom make the lower deck welcoming, spacious, and comfortable.
