Opis proizvođača (na engleskom)

The epitome of an ‘Open’ boat, the Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck is an innovatively designed dayboat with a completely open deck. Her ergonomics and the central driving position have been through very carefully to optimise all the features on board.  The wonderful amount of space allows up to eight people on board.

With her incisive looks, the Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck is oozing with character. The profiled lateral struts and elegantly designed windshield emphasise her sporty appearance. With an incredible amount of beam on deck she features generous spaces for this size of boat and boasts safe circulation on board. With a pronounced V-shaped bow, the almost vertical surfaces of her hull deflect water perfectly. An orange line common to all the Flyer SPACEdeck’s is the signature mark of this model.
