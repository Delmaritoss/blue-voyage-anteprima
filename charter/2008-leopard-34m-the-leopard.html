Opis proizvođača (na engleskom)

Fast. Spacious. Stabilized Elegant. Refitted. Available now. The only stabilized, 5 Cabins, 42 Knots Top Speed Leopard 34m

The superb M/Y THE LEOPARD underwent a major interior and exterior refit in 2018 to enhance her contemporary and stylish design created by Andrea Bacigalupo with an additional 5th double cabin (1 Master, 1 Double, 2 VIP, 1 Twin) on the main deck where in the original layout you find the Dining Saloon.

Accommodating now 11 guests in her elegant and comfortable 5 cabins embellished with classy and modern colors and fabrics, M/Y LEOPARD can cruise up to 12 guests providing spacious and luxurious comfort.

The spacious garage will surprise you as it can accommodate a Tender plus a Jet Ski at the same time while still allowing for extra toys.

All furnitures at the aft deck lounge are modular and can be removed to provide an open plan area for a dance floor or any other event setup.

On the front deck you have a beautiful Sun Lounge with flexible table arrangement, Shell like Sun Shading and easy access stairs.

This yacht is available for sale immediately and offers the full package with 5 Cabins, Gyro stabilization, high speed yet economical overall fuel consumption per mile and is the perfect mediterranean fun cruiser.
