Opis proizvođača (na engleskom)

The adriatic’s modern eco-yacht, ‘life is good’ is a new 45m (147’8″) 100% croatian-made modern motor-sailer for charter.

Delivered in early 2022 in time to enjoy an enormously successful sell-out first season.

Instantly recognisable by her unique brushed-steel grey hull and superstructure, this yacht’s noteworthy feature is the huge alloy composite mast which stands 48m tall – by far the highest amongst her peers and at least 12m taller than her next best in class. This impressive single mast boosts sailing performance and houses over 200m2 of high-tech competition rigging at the main and fore sail. She is a full ocean-going yacht with an international licence.

Up on the top sundeck there is a vast amount of relaxation and breakout spaces. Towards the bow, the steps are leading up to a large jacuzzi; perfect to enjoy a soak whilst sipping your favourite cocktail served from the nearby fully-stocked wet bar. There are assorted sun loungers, seating areas, and an outdoor high fidelity music system to provide the soundtrack.

At full stern ‘Life is Good’ features an adjustable hydraulic swimming platform which can also be used for candle-lit dinners on that special occasion. This platform is an ideal launchpad to enjoy the rich offering of watersports available.

‘Life is Good’ is an excellent modern luxury sailing yacht packed with the latest features and perfect for an extended family or large group to enjoy a week on the water at their own pace. She has a vastly experienced Captain who heads an attentive crew of 8 and an award-winning chef.

Stepping aboard the spacious rear deck, guests are greeted by the huge divan sofas at the stern fronted by an ample outside dining table with space for 14 guests.

Moving into the airy salon the feeling is of refined decadence with tasteful under-stated soft furnishing and interior styling provided by the renowned Iterijeri and XIMAR. A pop-up entertainment island in the main salon features 2 retractable flatscreen TVs, a fully staffed cocktail bar adjacent to the quartz dining table.

Below deck features 6 spacious staterooms decorated to a high standard with seated vanity bars and walk-in storage features. The fore and aft each feature a full-beam Master cabin with walk-around king-size beds and walk-in closets, each with distressed marble en-suites and shower boxes.

The four remaining large VIP cabins feature queen-size beds (2 convertible to twin rooms) with fully-appointed en-suites and generous windows to enjoy the shifting horizons. There is also a small double nanny cabin on the upper deck.
